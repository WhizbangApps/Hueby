  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="updated">Last updated: [RELEASE DATE: owner to set]</p>

  <p>Hueby ("the app") is developed by Mark Wong ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app accesses, what it sends, and the choices you have.</p>

  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Your photos never leave your device, and we never know who you are.</strong>
    <p>Hueby has no accounts and no sign-in. If you allow it, the app sends anonymous counts of how it is used. They are never linked to you, and never used for advertising.</p>
  </div>

  <h2>Your Photos</h2>

  <div class="permission-list">
    <div class="permission-item">
      <div class="permission-icon">🖼️</div>
      <div>
        <strong>Photos and camera</strong>
        <p>Import Studio turns a photo into a picture you can colour. Photos are chosen through Apple's system photo picker, so the app is never given access to your library as a whole. Only the single image you select is handed to it. If you take a photo with the camera, it is used the same way and is not saved to your photo library. Converting the image, and Reimagine, which uses Apple's Image Playground, happen on your device. A photo is never uploaded by Hueby and is never part of the usage data described below.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <p>You can delete an imported picture from inside the app at any time.</p>

  <h2>What Stays On Your Device</h2>

  <p>Everything the app keeps for you, it keeps locally:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>Pictures you are part-way through colouring, and the pictures you have finished.</li>
    <li>Pictures you have imported.</li>
    <li>The date of your last free import.</li>
    <li>Your settings, such as sound, haptics and reminders.</li>
  </ul>

  <p>Two small things are kept in your own iCloud key-value storage, so they follow you to your other devices: the Special Seasonal Packs you have kept, and the Finds you have bought. That storage belongs to your Apple Account. We have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes what is on the device. If your device is included in an iCloud or computer backup, that backup is Apple's and yours.</p>

  <h2>Usage Data</h2>

  <p>To learn which parts of Hueby are used and which are not, the app can send anonymous counts of named events. It is only ever these:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>When you open the app, and whether it is the first time.</li>
    <li>When you start and finish a picture, with the Pack and picture it came from. For an imported picture, only that it was an import and how big it is.</li>
    <li>When you load a photo into Import Studio, which Looks you try, and the settings of the pictures you create.</li>
    <li>When you open the Store or the Studio plans, which plan you choose, and how a purchase or a restore turned out.</li>
    <li>When you use your free import, and when you see or tap the offer for a Pack after finishing a picture.</li>
  </ul>

  <p>Each count also carries the app and iOS version, the device model, your language, the country of your App Store storefront, and whether you have the Studio and which plan. It never includes your photos, the names you give pictures, your email, your Apple Account, your contacts, your location, or your advertising identifier. Your IP address is not used to work out where you are.</p>

  <p>The counts carry a random ID made for this install. It is not your Apple ID or your device's identifier, it is not joined with any other data, and it is replaced with a new one if you switch sharing off and on again. We do not use the counts for advertising, we do not combine them with other data, and we do not sell them.</p>

  <p>In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Hueby asks you once, and sends nothing unless you say yes. Everywhere else, sharing is on by default, on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the app, and you can turn it off. In every country, <strong>Settings, then Privacy, then Share usage data</strong> turns it off at once.</p>

  <p>The counts are processed by Mixpanel, Inc. on our behalf, under its data processing agreement, and are stored in the European Union. They are kept for [RETENTION: owner to confirm].</p>

  <h2>In-App Purchases</h2>
  <p>Picture packs, the Painting Tools, packs of Finds, and the Studio subscription (and the one-time Studio some players bought earlier) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details. "Restore purchases" simply asks Apple what you already own. If you share usage data, the counts record the product and the outcome of a purchase, such as completed or cancelled, and nothing about you.</p>

  <h2>Other Requests</h2>
  <p>The app asks for one small public file from GitHub Pages, to learn about a sale on the Studio or the Painting Tools. The request carries no identifier and no information about you or your device. GitHub, like any website, can see the IP address a request comes from. We never receive it.</p>

  <h2>Notifications</h2>
  <p>Hueby sends two kinds of notification, both created on your device and sent by no server: a Daily picture reminder, if you turn it on, and a reminder two days before a Studio free trial ends, if you ask for it. You can turn either off in Settings.</p>

  <h2>Children</h2>
  <p>Hueby is a general-audience app, suitable for all ages, and is not in the App Store's Kids category. It has no accounts, and the usage counts described above are anonymous and never include a name, a photo or any other personal detail. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, nothing is sent until a player or their parent allows it.</p>

  <h2>Your Rights</h2>
  <p>The usage counts are tied to a random ID that we cannot connect to you, so we cannot look up, export or delete the counts of one person. You can stop them at any time with the Settings switch, and a new ID is made if you switch them back on. We hold no other data about you. Everything else Hueby stores is on your device and under your control: reset a picture, delete an import, or delete the app to remove it all. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may withdraw your consent at any time in the same place, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.</p>

  <h2>Changes</h2>
  <p>If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.</p>
